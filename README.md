# Remix of EAVORTEX

Build a complete, premium forex technology platform called EA VORTEX.



REFERENCE WEBSITE:

https://ea-nexus.co.za/



Use the reference website as inspiration for the overall interface, layout, futuristic trading aesthetic, dashboard presentation, and visual hierarchy. Create an original EA VORTEX identity with its own logo, text, and design details.



Do not use the EA Nexus name or logo anywhere in the product.



==================================================

1. BRAND IDENTITY AND VISUAL DESIGN

==================================================



Brand name: EA VORTEX



Tagline: "Power Your Trading."



Create a premium, futuristic forex platform with a professional trading-technology aesthetic.



COLOUR PALETTE:

- Main background: almost-black (#080808).

- Secondary background: dark charcoal (#111111).

- Card background: #151515.

- Primary accent: vivid red (#FF3030).

- Secondary accent: darker red (#B5121B).

- Primary text: white (#FFFFFF).

- Secondary text: muted grey (#A0A0A0).

- Borders: subtle dark grey.



DESIGN DIRECTION:

- Premium fintech interface.

- Bold, modern typography.

- Strong headlines.

- Dark backgrounds with sharp red highlights.

- Subtle red glow effects.

- Clean, sophisticated cards.

- Minimal but effective animations.

- Smooth transitions.

- Professional candlestick charts.

- Subtle grid patterns and technical chart elements.

- Beautiful spacing and consistent alignment.



Use red selectively for buttons, active navigation, important indicators, borders, and highlighted words.



Avoid cartoon illustrations, excessive gradients, generic templates, and cluttered layouts.



The website should look like a serious, established trading technology company.

5. REAL AI CHART ANALYSIS

==================================================



Connect the scanner to an actual AI vision model through a secure backend.



When a user uploads a chart and clicks "SCAN CHART", the system must send the image and relevant selections to the AI service and display its actual response.



Analyse the chart for:



1. Market structure.

2. Overall trend.

3. Support and resistance zones.

4. Candlestick patterns.

5. Chart patterns.

6. Breakouts and possible false breakouts.

7. Potential reversal areas.

8. Bullish scenarios.

9. Bearish scenarios.

10. Relevant visible indicators.

11. Potential entry areas when supported by the chart.

12. Possible invalidation levels.

13. Potential target zones.

14. Risk-to-reward considerations.

15. Important uncertainties.



Do not fabricate technical indicators that are not visible.



Do not invent prices or exact levels when the screenshot does not provide enough information.



If the image is unclear, explain its limitations.



For GOLD, consider the chart's visible structure and volatility, but do not claim access to current gold prices unless a genuine live market-data integration exists.

6. AI SCANNER RESULTS PAGE

==================================================



Display the analysis in a sophisticated dashboard layout.



Create the following sections:



MARKET OVERVIEW

Summarise the instrument, timeframe, and visible market conditions.



TREND ANALYSIS

Describe the apparent trend and market structure.



SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE

Identify visible price zones when the image provides sufficient information.



CHART PATTERNS

Describe identifiable patterns and explain their significance.



BULLISH SCENARIO

Explain conditions that could support an upward move.



BEARISH SCENARIO

Explain conditions that could support a downward move.



POTENTIAL TRADE SETUP

Where justified, describe a hypothetical entry area, invalidation level, possible targets, and relevant risk considerations.



RISK ANALYSIS

Highlight uncertainty, volatility, and possible alternative outcomes.



AI SUMMARY

Provide a short, easy-to-understand conclusion.



Use red for bearish highlights, green for bullish highlights, and neutral colours for mixed or uncertain conditions.



Include buttons:

- Scan Another Chart

- Save Analysis

- Copy Summary

- View Scan History



Label potential setups as hypothetical scenarios. Never guarantee profits or present AI analysis as certain.
